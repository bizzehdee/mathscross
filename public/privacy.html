MathsCross privacy policy

MathsCross collects no data about you. None at all.

Google Play requires every app to publish a privacy policy, even one that collects nothing. This is that policy, and it is short because there is very little to say.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, and no tracking.

The app does not ask for the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere even by mistake. You can check this yourself: the permission list on the Play listing is empty.

What is stored on your device

So that the game works and remembers where you were, the following is saved in the app's own storage on your device:

  • the puzzle you are part-way through, and your entries in it;
  • how long you have spent on it, and your undo history;
  • which puzzles you have completed, your best and typical times, and your daily streak;
  • your theme choice.

All of it stays on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, because there is nowhere for it to be sent. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, removes all of it permanently.

Accounts, adverts and payments

There are none. No sign-in, no adverts, no in-app purchases, and no third-party services of any kind are embedded in the app.

Children

MathsCross is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here rather than quietly substituted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's repository: github.com/bizzehdee/mathscross.
